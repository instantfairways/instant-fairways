04 — GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS?
WE'VE GOT ANSWERS.

Is Instant Fairways taking bookings now?

We're currently collecting expressions of interest ahead of launch. Submitting the form does not confirm a booking.

Where will you operate?

We're planning to serve Perth and surrounding areas. Tell us your suburb so we can assess demand and feasibility.

Do guests need to know how to play golf?

Not at all. We're designing the experience for golfers and first-timers alike.

How much space will the setup need?

Final dimensions and site requirements depend on the equipment selected. We'll share accurate requirements once the setup is confirmed.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your event, location and setup requirements. As a guide, we’re planning hire from $300 per hour, or a 4-hour package for $1,000 ($250 per hour). Custom packages may be available for larger events and brand activations. These are indicative prices, not confirmed booking rates, and will be finalised before launch.
