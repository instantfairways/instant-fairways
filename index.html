GOT
QUESTIONS?

What is a mobile golf simulator? +

It's a golf simulator experience brought to your event. Guests hit real golf shots into a simulator screen and enjoy an interactive virtual golf experience.

Do guests need golf experience? +

Not at all. The experience is designed to be enjoyed by golfers and first-timers alike.

What types of events do you cater for? +

Corporate functions, private parties, birthdays, bucks parties, weddings, golf days, fundraisers and business activations.

Where do you operate? +

Instant Fairways is focused on Perth and surrounding areas in Western Australia. Send through your event suburb and we'll discuss your location.

How much space do we need? +

Space requirements depend on the setup and venue. Include your venue details in the enquiry and we'll discuss what's suitable.

How much does it cost? +

Pricing depends on the event, location and hire requirements. Get in touch for a tailored quote.
